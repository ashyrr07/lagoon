Lagoon turns the camera cutout on your phone into a Dynamic Island: a small black pill that wraps the camera and shows what matters right now, then gets out of the way.

What it shows
• Music: album art, title and artist, play/pause, next and previous, a seek bar. Tap to open the player.
• Calls: who is calling with answer and decline, then a call timer and hang up.
• Timers and stopwatches from your Clock app, plus the next alarm.
• Charging: level and charging power when you plug in, low and full battery alerts.
• Earbuds and other Bluetooth devices connecting, with battery level where the device reports it.
• Turn-by-turn navigation from Google Maps or Waze.
• Notifications from the apps you choose, with the full text, actions and inline reply when expanded.

Quick controls
Long-press the island for a control deck you arrange yourself: torch, Do Not Disturb, rotation lock, brightness and volume sliders, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth panels, camera, app shortcuts and small widgets.

Fits every phone
Lagoon reads the cutout position from the system and lets you fine-tune position, size and corner radius by hand, so it fits a centred hole, a corner hole or a notch on any brand.

Private by design
Lagoon has no INTERNET permission. It cannot send anything anywhere: no analytics, no crash reporting, no accounts, no ads. Notification access is used only to show your notifications and media on the island. The source code is public under the GPL-3.0 licence.

Two modes
• Standard mode needs only "Display over other apps" and Notification access. It is the default and does not use any accessibility service.
• Enhanced mode is optional and off by default. It uses an accessibility service so that the whole pill is tappable, the island can appear on the lock screen and the control deck can take screenshots, lock the screen or open recents. The service does not read screen content. You can turn it off at any time in the system Accessibility settings.

Lagoon is free, has no paid tier and never will. If something does not work on your phone, open an issue on GitHub with your device model and Android version.
